Company name: FLEX (The Flex Company)

What is your company going to make?
FLEX is a disposable feminine hygiene product. It's shaped like a diaphragm, used for mess-free period sex, and can be worn in place of traditional menstrual products.

The menstrual products we use today were created in the 1930s. They're uncomfortable, disruptive, and inconvenient. 91% of women say they want alternatives, yet the $15B market for feminine hygiene continues to grow.

FLEX is worn internally, designed for 12 hours of wear, and has the added benefit of mess-free period sex. It is disposable, is not linked to toxic shock syndrome, and is so comfortable that women tell us they forget they're on their period when wearing it.

Why did you pick this idea?
Three reasons:

First, personal experience. Like many women, I've spent a quarter of my life in discomfort caused by traditional menstrual products. I've also been bothered by the social stigma surrounding menstruation and period sex. Couples miss out on 23% more opportunities for sex, often during the time women want it most.

Second, market timing. NPR called 2015 the Year of the Period. Women are trying new products like menstrual cups and period panties, but these have come with new frustrations. 91% of women want a tampon alternative.

Third, product foundation. We discovered an old patent for a product neglected by a small private drug company. It has 20 years of post-clinical research to draw from, letting us move quickly, streamline the FDA process, and avoid expensive design iterations. We're improving it with better fit, materials, and US manufacturing.

What's new about FLEX?
Direct-to-consumer e-commerce and subscription distribution
Chic design, high-end packaging, differentiated branding aimed at millennials
Wearable up to 12 hours, not linked to TSS
Allows mess-free intercourse, enabling 23% more sex opportunities
Fewer changes means less environmental waste

Key difference from menstrual cups: cups block the vaginal canal, must be rinsed and reused in public restrooms, are difficult to insert and remove, and require a 5-10 year behavior change. FLEX is disposable and addresses these barriers.

Competitors
Most feared: large consumer goods companies (P&G, Kimberly-Clark, Playtex, Energizer). They have resources and channel relationships but lack a brand voice that resonates with millennials.

Also watching: Softcup (similar product design, low awareness), DivaCup and Lunette (menstrual cups), and millennial-focused startups like LOLA, Cora, THiNX, and Dear Kate.

What do you understand about your business that others don't?
Women and men love our branding. 25% of signups are from men. We've found a way to stand out among cheap pink-and-purple packaging of traditional products, and our mess-free period sex messaging has been an effective hook for first-time trial.

Once women try FLEX, they want to switch permanently:
FLEX is so comfortable that women forget they're on their period
Requires changing only once every 12 hours vs 4-5 tampon changes per day
Easy to use

Most menstrual product purchases happen in drug stores, which don't facilitate discovery. Millennials read and shop online. Our e-commerce platform educates them with peer-generated reviews. On average, every person who signs up recommends FLEX to at least one additional person.

Business Model
Monthly subscriptions sold through our own e-commerce website with free shipping for subscribers.

Global feminine hygiene market is over $15B and growing. US tampon market is $4B. We aim to capture $3M in revenue in the first two years and see ourselves as a $100M company in 5 years, similar to Dollar Shave Club.

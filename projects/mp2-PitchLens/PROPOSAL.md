## My Project Proposal

**What I'm building:** A tool that analyzes the language patterns in successful YC startup 
application essays to identify what writing traits the most successful pitches share.

**Why I chose this:** I'm interested in entrepreneurship and want to understand what 
language and framing make a compelling startup pitch — insights I can apply to my own 
ideas.

**Core features:**
- Load and clean text from multiple pitch essays
- Count word frequencies and identify the most common words per essay
- Measure vocabulary richness (unique word ratio) across essays
- Detect "confidence language" (e.g., "will", "proven", "launched") vs. hedging words
- Visualize findings with bar charts comparing essays

**What I don't know yet:**
- How to reliably find enough public YC essays in plain text format
- How to best measure "confidence" in language quantitatively
- Whether stop-word removal will meaningfully change the results
